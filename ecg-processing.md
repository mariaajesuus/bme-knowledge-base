# ECG Signal Processing

Electrocardiography (ECG) measures electrical activity generated by heart muscle depolarizations.

## Processing Pipeline
- Signal Acquisition: Differential biological electrodes.
- Filtering: Removal of powerline interference and baseline wander.
- Waveform Analysis: Identification of P, Q, R, S, and T waves.

## Detection Algorithms
- Pan-Tompkins: QRS-complex detection method based on bandpass filtering and window integration.
- Threshold Detection: Dynamic amplitude tracking for heart rate variability calculation.
